# 🚀 NxtWave Growth Challenge — AI Workshop Registration Campaign

A 7-day growth strategy to target **500 final-year engineering student registrations** for NxtWave’s free online workshop, **“Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes.”**

**Submitted by:** Rakshitha Joycey  
**Student ID:** AP23110011475

---

## 📌 Overview

This project was created as part of the NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge. The objective is to develop a practical, measurable, and budget-conscious campaign to attract final-year engineering students to a free AI workshop.

The challenge focuses on understanding the target audience, choosing effective marketing channels, using AI thoughtfully, and building a working asset to support the campaign.

### 🎯 Challenge Constraints

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Registration target | 500 students |
| Campaign duration | 7 days |
| Maximum budget | ₹2,000 |
| Target audience | Final-year engineering students |
| Workshop | Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes |

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the needs and motivations of final-year engineering students.
- Design a focused marketing campaign using a small budget.
- Set measurable registration targets for each channel.
- Develop a registration and referral tracking solution.
- Use AI to support decision-making while applying independent judgment.
- Identify improvements through tracking and feedback.

---

## 👥 Target Audience

The primary audience is final-year engineering students, especially those interested in AI, project development, placements, and internships.

### 💡 Why Would Students Register?

- 🎁 The workshop is free and beginner-friendly.
- 🤖 It offers a practical introduction to building an AI project.
- ⏱️ It takes only 60 minutes.
- 🛠️ It provides an opportunity to explore AI without requiring prior project-building experience.

---

## 📈 Growth Strategy

The campaign prioritises four marketing channels instead of spreading limited resources across too many activities.

| Marketing Channel | Target Registrations | Strategy |
|---|---:|---|
| 🏫 College clubs and class representatives | 220 | Reach students through trusted campus networks |
| 💬 WhatsApp and Telegram groups | 160 | Share concise workshop invitations in relevant groups |
| 📧 Email and student communities | 70 | Reach additional students through targeted communication |
| 🤝 Peer referrals | 50 | Encourage registered students to invite friends |
| **🎯 Total** | **500** | **Target registrations** |

> **Note:** These figures are planning targets, not guaranteed results. Actual performance must be measured during the campaign.

## 📅 Seven-Day Execution Plan

| Day | Planned Activity |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Prepare the registration form, campaign creatives, and tracking sheet. |
| **Day 2** | Connect with college clubs and class representatives. |
| **Day 3** | Share the campaign in relevant student communities and review results. |
| **Day 4** | Introduce referral tracking and encourage peer sharing. |
| **Day 5** | Focus on the channels producing better results. |
| **Day 6** | Send a reminder with confirmed workshop details. |
| **Day 7** | Complete the final promotion and review unique registrations. |

## 🛠️ Working Asset: Registration and Referral Tracker

The proposed working asset is a registration and referral tracker designed to monitor campaign performance.

### ✨ Planned Features

- 📍 Track registrations by marketing channel.
- 📆 Record daily registration totals.
- 🔗 Attribute referrals using source or referral codes.
- 🔍 Identify duplicate or incomplete entries.
- 🎯 Monitor progress towards the 500-registration target.
- 📊 Help determine which channels deserve more attention.

## 📏 Measurement and Evaluation

The campaign would be evaluated using the following metrics:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ✅ Unique registrations | Count valid, deduplicated sign-ups |
| 📍 Registrations by source | Measure the contribution of each marketing channel |
| 📆 Daily registration pace | Track the number of new registrations per day |
| 🤝 Referral performance | Measure registrations attributed to peer sharing |
| 🔍 Duplicate rate | Identify entries that should not count as separate registrations |

The results would guide decisions throughout the seven-day campaign.

## 🤖 How AI Was Used

AI was used as a brainstorming and planning aid to explore marketing channels, budget allocation, and potential working assets.

The final approach prioritises a smaller number of channels, measurable targets, and a tracking solution rather than adopting every suggestion without evaluation.

The AI Learning Notes document the suggestions considered, changes made, and decisions rejected.

## 📚 Key Learnings

- 🎯 Growth campaigns need a clearly defined audience and measurable goals.
- 💰 Limited budgets require prioritisation.
- 📈 Registrations are more meaningful than views or impressions alone.
- 📊 Tracking helps identify which channels deserve additional effort.
- 🤖 AI can support planning, but decisions should be evaluated against real constraints.
